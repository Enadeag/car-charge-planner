The first release of the Android app: the same planner as the desktop app, on your phone. Set-up, a dashboard with a tab for each day of the plan, trips and their advice, grid advice, away periods, Settings with seven of the desktop's pages, and a notice before each charge window, also while the app is closed. The API keys are kept encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore. Needs Android 10 or newer; in English and Dutch.
