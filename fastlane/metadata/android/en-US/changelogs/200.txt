A smaller and more guarded build: the APK is about 9 MB instead of 30 MB, and every release is built twice and signed only when both builds are the same. The app targets Android 17 and has its own icon. Your settings now move to your next phone on Android 10 and 11 too. The battery card no longer shows a level the car never reaches. A server address must start with https://. On Android 17 and later the app cannot reach a forecast server on your home network.
