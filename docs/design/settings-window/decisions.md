# The desktop's Settings window: decisions

What the desktop's Settings and set-up do with a typed number, beside [ADR 0011](../../adr/0011-engine-says-what-a-setting-may-be.md). The Android app's forms are in [android/settings.md](../android/settings.md) and the engine's side in [engine/decisions.md](../engine/decisions.md).

### Numbers and the engine's rules

- A number field holds what was loaded or typed, as it is: nothing is brought into a range, and the field's own value factory (`NumberInput`) does not clamp, where JavaFX's does. A stored value within the engine's rules shows as it is, at the field's decimals, and is saved back exactly when the owner leaves it alone, also when another page or the language is saved (#317).
- The arrows and the arrow keys stop at the engine's bound, rounded inward to what the field shows (621 mi, 0.32 to 62.13 mi/kWh, 1% for a margin of more than 0, 99% for losses below 100%); a setting without a highest value has no stop. A field starts at 0 before it is loaded, or at its lowest value when 0 is not allowed (#317).
- A field reads what was typed as the Android app does: a decimal point or comma in either language, a leading minus, ASCII digits, spaces around it left out, and nothing else; it writes its number without a thousands separator, so what it shows reads back the same (#317).
- On Save and on set-up's Next each field first says what is wrong with its text: "Type a number." for no number, "Type a whole number, without decimals." for decimals in a field of amps or minutes, and the general out-of-range text for a whole number too long for one. Only when every text is a number do the engine's records check the values, and the first value a record refuses shows under its field: one problem per record, as before (#317).
- A value the engine refuses as out of range says the limit in the field's unit and the owner's number format, in one of five sentences: from … to …, … or more, more than …, more than … and at most …, and … or more and below …. An end that is allowed is named rounded inward, so a value the text names is accepted; an end that is not allowed is named as it is (#317).
- A problem goes as soon as its field is edited, or another value is put in it; a text that is not a number stays as typed until then (#317).
- A field under a fold that shows a problem opens the fold and holds it open until the next check finds none: the charger's values, the Car and house page's trip surcharge and household use, the Planning page's margin, import tolerance and shortest window, a panel array's losses, and the Location page's coordinates (#317).
